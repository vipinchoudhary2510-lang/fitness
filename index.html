Common questions

Is this available outside India?

Yes. Clients are coached worldwide over WhatsApp, so support reaches you whatever your time zone.

How is the program delivered?

Everything arrives on WhatsApp — exercise videos demonstrated by me, your diet plan, and recipe videos so nothing is left to guesswork.

Does the plan stay the same every month?

No. Loads, exercises and meals are updated each month so progress keeps moving and training never goes stale.

What if I'm short on equipment or carrying an injury?

The plan is adjusted around you. You'll get alternative movements that fit what you have and work around the injury rather than through it.

What if I'm a complete beginner?

Beginners are welcome. You'll get coaching on form and technique, and a starting point matched to your current level.

What exactly is the Inner Circle?

It's personalised coaching: custom training and meals, regular check-ins, progress tracking, and direct access to me between sessions.
